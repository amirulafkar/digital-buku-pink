# Interview guides (Phase 0, Gate G0)

Purpose: learn what actually goes wrong at antenatal check-in and queueing. Not a sales pitch. Do not describe the app as finished or MOH-approved. Say it is a fictional-data prototype, not MOH-approved.

Rules for every interview:
- Record roles and clinic codes only. No names, IC numbers, phone numbers, addresses or patient details. If someone volunteers a patient detail, do not write it down.
- Ask open questions first. Ask about the problem before mentioning any solution.
- Do not promise anything (pilot, data, support, timeline). Do not claim any clinic has agreed to anything.
- Log each answer in `g0-evidence.md` within 24 hours, as what was said, not what you hoped.
- Malay wording is a draft. **Needs native review** before first use.

---

## (a) Klinik Kesihatan nurses and staff (counter, queue, antenatal)

Target: 5 staff across 2+ clinics (G0 check 2). Ask unprompted first.

1. Tell me about a normal antenatal morning, from the moment a mother arrives to when she sees the nurse.
   - *Bahasa:* Ceritakan tentang satu pagi biasa di klinik antenatal, dari masa ibu tiba sehingga dia berjumpa jururawat.
2. What goes wrong most often at check-in or in the queue?
   - *Bahasa:* Apa yang paling kerap tersasar semasa pendaftaran atau dalam barisan?
3. Which steps are on paper, and who writes them? Where do the papers go?
   - *Bahasa:* Langkah mana yang masih guna kertas, siapa yang menulisnya, dan ke mana kertas itu pergi?
4. When a mother is called out of order, or a room is free, how is that decided and who decides?
   - *Bahasa:* Apabila ibu dipanggil tidak mengikut giliran, atau bilik kosong, bagaimana keputusan dibuat dan siapa yang membuatnya?
5. Do you already use a queue system (for example CCMS or a counter number system)? What does it do well, and what does it not cover?
   - *Bahasa:* Adakah klinik sudah guna sistem giliran (contoh CCMS atau sistem nombor kaunter)? Apa yang ia buat dengan baik, dan apa yang tidak diliputinya?
6. How long does a typical check-in take, and what do you think takes the most time?
   - *Bahasa:* Berapa lama biasanya satu pendaftaran, dan apa yang Encik/Puan rasa paling memakan masa?
7. If you could change one thing about check-in or the queue tomorrow, what would it be? (Ask this last. Do not suggest answers.)
   - *Bahasa:* Jika boleh tukar satu perkara tentang pendaftaran atau giliran esok, apakah itu?
8. Who else should I talk to about how this is done? Do not ask for names; ask for roles.
   - *Bahasa:* Siapa lagi patut saya tanya tentang cara kerja ini? (Minta jawatan, bukan nama.)

Record for each: role, clinic code, date, what was said, and whether the problem was named unprompted (yes/no). Note the evidence line number in `g0-evidence.md`.

## (b) Clinic manager or gatekeeper (access)

Purpose: G0 checks 1 and 3. Find out who can authorise observation and what approval a third-party tool needs. Do not ask them to agree on the spot. Ask for the next step in writing.

1. Who decides whether an outside person can observe the check-in or queue? What is their role?
   - *Bahasa:* Siapa yang memutuskan sama ada orang luar boleh memerhati pendaftaran atau giliran? Apakah jawatan mereka?
2. Has the clinic ever allowed an outside observer or a researcher before? What was the process?
   - *Bahasa:* Pernahkah klinik membenarkan pemerhati atau penyelidik luar sebelum ini? Apakah prosesnya?
3. If a tool were used in the clinic, what approval would it need? Which office or form?
   - *Bahasa:* Jika sebuah alat digunakan di klinik, apakah kelulusan yang diperlukan? Pejabat atau borang mana?
4. Would a pilot with no patient names or identifiers be allowed? What would it need to show?
   - *Bahasa:* Adakah projek perintis tanpa nama atau pengenalan pesakit dibenarkan? Apa yang perlu ditunjukkan?
5. Who would be accountable if data were misused? Who would you call at 2 am if the system failed?
   - *Bahasa:* Siapa yang bertanggungjawab jika data disalahguna? Siapa yang dihubungi jam 2 pagi jika sistem gagal?
6. Could you confirm the answers to questions 1 to 4 in writing (email or letter)? Who should I send the request to?
   - *Bahasa:* Bolehkah jawapan soalan 1 hingga 4 disahkan secara bertulis (e-mel atau surat)? Kepada siapa permintaan patut saya hantar?

Record: role, clinic or office code, date, the exact words used for any "yes", "maybe" or "no", and whether a written next step was offered. **A polite "maybe" is recorded as no.**

## (c) Mothers (non-clinical, no personal data)

Purpose: learn how mothers experience waiting and check-in. Not a clinical interview. Do not ask about pregnancy history, health conditions, test results, names, IC numbers or contact details. Do not interview mothers inside the clinic without the clinic's written permission. Keep it to 10 minutes or less.

1. How do you usually arrive at the clinic and check in? What do you do first?
   - *Bahasa:* Bagaimana biasanya Puan sampai ke klinik dan mendaftar? Apa yang Puan buat dahulu?
2. While you wait, what do you find confusing or tiring?
   - *Bahasa:* Semasa menunggu, apa yang Puan rasa mengelirukan atau meletihkan?
3. Do you know how long you are likely to wait? How do you find out?
   - *Bahasa:* Adakah Puan tahu anggaran masa menunggu? Bagaimana Puan tahu?
4. Do you use your phone during your clinic visit? For what?
   - *Bahasa:* Adakah Puan guna telefon semasa lawatan klinik? Untuk apa?
5. If you could know one thing while waiting, what would it be?
   - *Bahasa:* Jika Puan boleh tahu satu perkara semasa menunggu, apakah itu?

Record: age band only if volunteered (for example 20s, 30s), clinic code, date and what was said. Do not record anything that identifies the mother.
